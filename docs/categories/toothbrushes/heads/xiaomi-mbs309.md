## Notes

Xiaomi names the Soft replacement head MBS309. The Oscillation Pro FAQ
names the Clean and Soft heads and excludes T300, T500, T501 and T302.
The Servo-oscillation Pro specification explicitly includes the same named
replacement-head products. Fit claims are manufacturer claims, not measured
interchangeability or a general claim about every Xiaomi head.
