# Robot vacuums

Working name: **OpenVac**. Replacement compatibility is recorded separately for each consumable class.

## Find a replacement

- [Robot vacuums](units/index.md)
- [Main brushes](main-brushes/index.md)
- [Side brushes](side-brushes/index.md)
- [Dust filters](filters/index.md)
- [Mop pads](mop-pads/index.md)
- [Dust bags](dust-bags/index.md)
- [Roller mops](roller-mops/index.md)

A shared filter does not imply a shared brush, mop or dock bag. Look up the exact device and replacement part, including suffixes such as Pro, Max, Ultra and Roller. Dock consumables can depend on the dock supplied with a particular model.

The graph follows explicit manufacturer fit lists. If a product title and its detailed fit list disagree, the disputed model is left out until the conflict can be resolved. A missing link means no verified claim is recorded here.

## Status

**Reference layer only.** Xiaomi, Roborock and Dreame entries document manufacturer replacement claims. Mount profiles remain unmeasured, and no cross-brand compatibility is established from appearance or similar model names.
